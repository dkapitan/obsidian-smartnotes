#### Highlights

> Coordination-avoiding data systems We have now made two interesting observations:

> Dataflow systems can maintain integrity guarantees on derived data without atomic commit, linearizability, or synchronous cross-shard coordination.

> Although strict uniqueness constraints require timeliness and coordination, many applications are fine with loose constraints that may be temporarily vio‐ lated and fixed up later, as long as integrity is preserved throughout. Taken together, these observations mean that dataflow systems can provide data management services for many applications without requiring coordination, while still giving strong integrity guarantees. Such coordination-avoiding data systems have a lot of appeal; they can achieve better performance and fault tolerance than systems that need to perform synchronous coordination [45].
