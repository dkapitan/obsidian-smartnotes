Add and change hotkeys in the Settings. 

Alt+Shift+[Left Key] = Toggle Left Sidebar
Alt+Shift+[Right Key] = Toggle Right Sidebar

#### Citations/Zotero
Ctrl+Shift+E = Insert link to new literature note (added in the background)
Ctrl+Shift+I = Insert content of literature note

#### Hotkeys++
Ctrl+M = Toggle between Todo/Done/Bulletpoint (already activated after installing the plugin)
