
> [!Info] What is this page?
> This is an example of a starting page in the early stages when all your main topics fit nicely on one page. 

> [!Note] What do I do with it?
> Here you can give yourself an overview over your topics, sorted by areas of interest. 
>  From here you can link to those permanent notes that are the best "entry points" into a topic. In the beginning these might be simply regular permanent notes, later these will usually be Hubs or even MOC's. 
>  When the simple starting page becomes too complex, it makes sense to give each area of interest and the projects their own page. [[Starting Page (later stages)|Here]] is an example of a simpler starting page with more complexity underneath.

> [!FAQ] Why the three digit numbers?
> By giving each area of interest a three digit number you can easily categorize hubs and projects later. You can, for example, write on a project note simply: "area::100" and dataview is able to create a list of projects sorted by area of interest. And if you want to break it further down later, you can create sub-areas called "110", "120" etc.
